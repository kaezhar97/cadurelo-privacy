Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Cadurelo is a focus timer provided by Octavio Avila Cardet. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, contact us, or visit this page.

Cadurelo uses your information to provide your account, categories, timer, and settings. The current app has no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, purchases, or subscriptions. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Information you provide and create

  • Account information: Sign in with Apple supplies an account identifier and may provide your name and email address. If you choose Hide My Email, we receive Apple's relay address instead of your personal email. Cadurelo does not receive your Apple Account password.
  • Profile: your display name or username and, if you choose to add one, your profile photo. The system photo picker lets you choose a photo; Cadurelo does not upload your entire photo library.
  • Work and settings: category names, descriptions, icons, colors, goals, progress counts and ordering; your selected category, timer state and durations; tutorial completion/replay preferences; and activity timestamps used to restore your timer and apply inactivity behavior.
  • Support and feedback: information you choose to send when requesting help or reporting a problem, such as your email address, message, screenshots, device model, or iOS version.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, save and synchronize your data, restore your timer, provide the tutorial and notifications, respond to support requests, protect the service, and carry out account deletion. Authentication tokens maintain your session. Service infrastructure may process connection and error logs, including IP addresses and request metadata, to operate and secure the service.

Apple, Supabase, and local storage

Apple provides Sign in with Apple. Supabase provides Cadurelo's account authentication, database, profile-image storage, and server functions. These services process the information needed to provide those features. The current app has no other configured external data integrations.

Some account data and session information are also stored on your device so the app can restore your session and work with cached data. Data sent to the backend uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access controls restrict account data to the corresponding account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Apple and Supabase operate infrastructure that may process information outside your country. Their own service policies also apply: Apple Privacy Policy and Supabase Privacy Policy.

Notifications and TestFlight

With your permission, Cadurelo schedules local timer notifications on your device. The app does not send your notification schedule to a separate push-notification provider. You can change notification and sound permissions in iPhone Settings.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple processes beta participation, diagnostics, and feedback under its TestFlight terms and privacy information. Apple may make crash reports and feedback available to the developer. Cadurelo does not include a separate third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK.

Retention and deletion

Your saved account data is kept to provide the service until you remove it or delete your account. You can delete individual categories, change your profile and settings, and delete your account from Profile → Delete account. To begin deletion, Cadurelo's server requires recently verified authorization with the same Apple Account, tied to your signed-in session. This verification expires after a short period. If it expires before deletion begins, you will need to verify with Apple again. Once deletion has begun, interrupted cleanup can be retried without repeating Apple verification; you must still be signed in to the account being deleted.

Account deletion removes your account, associated profile, categories, settings, timer data, and profile image from Cadurelo's active service. The deletion process also revokes the app's Apple authorization. In Cadurelo 1.0.1 and later, after successful deletion, the app removes that account's saved timer checkpoint from the device used for deletion. Interrupted device cleanup may finish when the app next opens. This does not erase caches on other devices or copies in service-provider backups and operational logs.

If deletion cannot finish because of a network or service error, the app shows a retry path; the request is not treated as successfully completed until the deletion operation succeeds. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete your server account.

Copies in service-provider backups and operational logs may remain for the provider's applicable retention periods. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve your request and keep a record of the response. These copies are not necessarily removed immediately when an account is deleted.

Your choices

You can use Apple's Hide My Email, leave the profile photo unset, edit your saved data, disable notifications, or delete your account. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account. Privacy rights available to you depend on where you live.

Cadurelo is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us.

This website and policy changes

This public page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visitor information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page has no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded media, or contact form.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest update. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.

Support

Need help with Cadurelo? Email octaviocardet97@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of what happened. Screenshots are useful if they do not expose private information.

For timer-alert problems, mention whether a notification appeared, whether a sound played, and whether Silent mode or Focus was enabled. For beta feedback, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

To delete your account, open Profile → Delete account in Cadurelo and complete Apple reauthorization. If you cannot access the app, email us for help.
